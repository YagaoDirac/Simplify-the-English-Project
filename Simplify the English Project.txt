[[[[What's This

This is a new version of English. It simplified the grammar and standardized the spelling according to some major American accent.



[[[[Background

In recent years, a universal natural language that is easy to learn and easy to use is more needed.
English is the most widely used language with a lot good designs. Updating English seems to be the most intuitive idea.



[[[[Main Content

[[[Grammar Adjustments

No plural form.
(one person, two person, one apple, two apple.)

No third-person singular present tense. So you don't need to add s to the verbs.
(I do. He do.)

No past tense. The old past tense grammar means "this behavior has finished" now, which is the old "perfect tense".
No verb-ing form, use adverbial of time.
(I do today. I do yesterday.) for "I do today. I did yesterday."
(I did yesterday.) for "I have done yesterday."
(I do now.) for "I'm doing now."

Verbs only have original form and -ed form.
(read) reidid
(wrote, written) now only wraitid
(went, gone) now only gouid.
(did, done) now only duid.
Be-verb has only bi and biid.
(Spelling adjustment see below)



[[[Spelling Adjustment

New spelling is a pinyin style of one of the most popular American accent.
The upside of pinyin is, it maps pronunciation to spelling one to one, with very few exceptions. My target is to make a pinyin system for English with no exceptions and no confusions.

In new design, pronunciation of vowel letters in Alphabet also changed. 
A is similar to the a in "am, an, and, add"
E is similar to the e in "get"
I is similar to the i in "tick"
O is similar to the o in "got"
U is similar to the vowel in "wood" but short.
